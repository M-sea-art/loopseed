# Creative Director Example: 玄秘门派

## Creative North Star

A small wuxia sect that feels alive every time the player returns. The core fantasy is not building rooms; it is watching people, relationships, duties, and the mountain home evolve.

## Player Promise

The player should feel like a real sect leader:

- making choices that affect disciples;
- witnessing daily life;
- protecting a fragile but growing community.

## Identity Guard

Preserve:

- living characters;
- quiet wuxia atmosphere;
- meaningful events.

Avoid:

- empty management dashboards;
- generic resource spreadsheets;
- beautiful but lifeless scenes.

## Production Translation

Prioritize a playable vertical slice proving life, decisions, and consequences before expanding systems.
