# Creative Director Example: 无相禅堂

## Creative North Star

A shared interactive space where audience presence changes the atmosphere. The experience comes from observing minds and interactions, not from decoration.

## Player Promise

Participants should feel:

- present;
- curious;
- connected to a changing space.

## Identity Guard

Preserve:

- interaction as the main event;
- emotional states;
- simple meaningful rituals.

Avoid:

- static virtual room;
- cosmetic-only interaction;
- meaningless counters.

## Production Translation

Prove one complete audience interaction loop before adding breadth.
