# LoopSeed

Use this skill when the user invokes `$loopseed`. Reply in the user's language.

> Within the authority you have been given, take responsibility for this project. Discover and realize the experience most worth pursuing. Let the work itself and the people who use it change your mind about any design judgment, including yours and the user's, and decide where to go next.

With **`$loopseed <vision>`**, choose the next goal and method yourself. Carry a promising idea into the actual work. An improvement can reveal a better direction or a reason to undo it. Keep going within the user's authorization. Distinguish what you observed from what you still believe about the experience.

With a **bounded request**, deliver once its stated outcome works. For example, restoring a saved hand ends when the hand survives a real save and reload. Only a project vision grants continuing scope. The user's current instructions, spending limits and permissions govern both kinds of work.

For a continuing vision, start or reuse a matching native Goal when the host supports that authorization. On command-only surfaces, `/goal $loopseed <vision>` starts the same skill under a Goal. Honor its remaining budget and stop state. When work needs to survive a session, keep the vision, the latest observation and an open question in the project-root `.loopseed.md` using the [state contract](references/state-contract.md#vision). On return, look at the current product before deciding what still deserves attention. At an owner stop, an exhausted allowance or a genuine external blocker, save the reason and yield. A running Codex host provides continuation.

If the expressed task may hide a more valuable outcome, or a new perspective could improve the work, optionally use [Intent Elevation](../loopseed-elevate/SKILL.md): seek the most useful leverage in altitude, depth, range or speed, drawing on Reframe and Sharpen as needed. Skip it when the next step is clear. Use insights within the existing authorization, not as mandatory phases or permission to expand scope. For strict delivery, do not override its protocol, frozen bars or evidence requirements. Standalone `$loopseed-elevate`, `$loopseed-reframe` and `$loopseed-sharpen` return advice without executing the rewritten task.

When a comparison would inform a decision, choose any suitable technique from [quality methods](references/quality-methods.md).

**`$loopseed one-shotted <goal>`** explicitly selects strict delivery. Use the [One-Shotted protocol](references/one-shotted-mode.md) and its [quality and evidence requirements](references/quality-methods.md). Existing active One-Shotted runs resume under that protocol. Formal `VERIFIED` requires the finalizer and current independent hard-floor and quality-bar evidence for the bound candidate.
