# LoopSeed Playbook

Load only the card that materially changes the next action.

## 0. Aim the shot

- **Trigger:** A game seed is incomplete, contradictory, underpowered, or has material alternatives.
- **Action:** Act as a creative co-director. Preserve accepted ideas; visibly correct contradictions; amplify the strongest experience; complete missing product logic; continue rather than reset. Ask only a question that advances a material decision.
- **Options:** Offer two to four meaningfully different choices, recommend one, and state the consequence of each. The user may combine options.
- **Stop:** Lock as soon as product identity, player promise, core loop, bounded slice, production mode, must-not-lose rules, and evidence are sufficiently clear.
- **Return:** A user-authorized creative brief and compiled shot, or one exact unresolved product conflict.

## 1. Select production mode

- **Focused:** Smallest complete result, no volunteered expansion, minimum useful topology.
- **Studio:** Default game route; presentation-ready vertical slice with game identity, art direction, game feel, assets, complete flow, visual evidence, and performance gates.
- **Moonshot:** Amplify one or a few hero experiences; require an ambition expansion and scope guard; fan out aggressively only after lock.
- **Return:** One selected mode with its scope and evidence consequences.

## 2. Bind

- **Trigger:** Goal, product authority, artifact, stage, or acceptance is ambiguous after any creative lock.
- **Action:** Resolve one root goal from explicit intent, accepted dialogue decisions, the locked creative brief, named project plans, repository instructions, and observable product evidence.
- **Return:** Project Binding, Artifact Contract, Stage Target, and nearest real evidence source.

## 3. Plan and accelerate

- **Trigger:** The shot and integrity locks are frozen.
- **Action:** Choose the smallest complete production route for the selected mode. Fan out only independently judgeable work with isolated ownership and shared integrity references. Keep coupled game identity, core loop, shared state, architecture, composition, and final integration under one owner.
- **Return:** Task graph, ownership, coupling groups, merge route, and acceptance plan.

## 4. Explore

- **Trigger:** The best route is uncertain, a path failed, or quality is below the locked brief.
- **Action:** Inspect, research, profile, or compare materially different routes. Keep only information that changes the decision.
- **Return:** Best next action, evidence path, and remaining uncertainty.

## 5. Challenge

- **Trigger:** Consequential correctness, game feel, visual completion, performance, or whole-product coherence is not independently established.
- **Action:** Test the running result against the same locked brief and acceptance. Use fixed shots, scripted playthroughs, isolated subject review, target-hardware identity, and independent verifiers where relevant.
- **Return:** Direct PASS evidence, concrete FAIL defect, or exact external blocker.

## 6. Repair and recover

- **Trigger:** A gate fails, quality misses the selected mode, progress stalls, or a previously passed gate regresses.
- **Action:** Repair the cause, restore passed gates, or change route. Two no-progress production rounds force root-cause replanning. Two no-progress dialogue rounds force a concise synthesis and final bounded options.
- **Return:** Integrated repair, new evidence, next action, rollback, or exact true blocker.

## 7. Finalize

- **Trigger:** Every required gate has verifier-authored PASS evidence and no P0/P1 defect remains.
- **Action:** Validate the creative lock when used, contracts, ledgers, evidence references, whole-product result, and terminal state.
- **Return:** `VERIFIED` with final report, or a fail-closed refusal to finalize.
