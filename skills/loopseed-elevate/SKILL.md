# Intent Elevation / 升维

Treat the prompt as evidence of intent, not a ceiling on what can be achieved.

What does the user want to happen after this work succeeds? Inspect the real work and available evidence. Identify the most valuable opportunity to improve the outcome rather than automatically expanding the task. A clear, small request may already be the right one.

## Choose the leverage, not the ceremony

- **Altitude (高度):** Move from the requested action to the result it serves, then to a durable mechanism or mission only when justified. Ask what should happen next. A recurring task may become a self-sustaining workflow, but identify the access, feedback and maintenance it would require. Keep the creative identity and aesthetic intent intact; do not replace an artistic choice with an invented business objective.
- **Depth (深度):** Use the raw material, real tools and methods of leading practitioners. Challenge assumptions with evidence; attempt an informative, feasible experiment when it can resolve a choice. Read [Sharpen](../loopseed-sharpen/SKILL.md) when its four questions would help. Seek independent evidence, not expert-sounding prose.
- **Range (广度):** Describe what the work must accomplish as verbs rather than job titles or artifact nouns. Discover relevant expert vocabulary and transferable methods from adjacent fields, including where the analogy breaks. Read [Reframe](../loopseed-reframe/SKILL.md) when language or framing limits the solution. Choose real methods, not role-playing. Verify any sources used.
- **Speed (速度):** Shorten the time from intent to a verified useful outcome, not merely time to generate output. Reuse tools, reduce handoffs, and parallelize independent work only when doing so pays off. Never trade away required evidence for speed.

Use whichever one or two dimensions offer meaningful leverage, or none. Reframe may also reveal a better outcome; Sharpen may sharpen a proposed mechanism. Do not force a sequence, score every dimension, generate a fixed report, or inflate the goal just to demonstrate ambition. Set challenging concrete targets when meaningful, explore alternate routes before accepting apparent limits, and never claim an unsupported target is feasible.

## Authority and evidence

In standalone use (including the Chinese alias 升维 when the user explicitly requests this skill), return a concise diagnosis, a stronger rewritten request, the evidence and access it needs, and any material choice or approval. Offer alternatives only when they would change the decision. Do not implement the rewritten request unless the user authorizes execution. Keep the original intent visible and distinguish inference from evidence.

Inside an already authorized LoopSeed vision, apply the insight directly if it fits the delegated objective, permissions and budget. Carry useful hypotheses into the actual product; revise them from observations. Ask only when a materially different outcome, new access, cost or irreversible action exceeds authorization. For bounded work, deepen or broaden methods without turning it into a larger or continuing assignment. Never treat a higher abstraction level as permission. An explicit strict One-Shotted contract, including its frozen bars and evidence requirements, cannot be rewritten by this skill once bound.

Use [LoopSeed quality methods](../loopseed/references/quality-methods.md) when an external test, fresh critic or comparison is needed. Do not mistake a proposed experiment for an executed one or an inference for a verified result. Respect existing stop rules. Revisit the framing only when feedback makes it worth revisiting.
