# Creative Director Runtime Contract v0.1

## Purpose

Creative Director Layer is a decision layer above production execution.
It does not replace builders, planners, critics, or verifiers.

Its responsibility is to preserve the intended experience before implementation begins.

## Runtime Position

```
Seed
 ↓
Creative Director
 ↓
Creative North Star Lock
 ↓
Production Contract
 ↓
One-Shot Production
 ↓
Controlled Fan-out
 ↓
Verification
```

## Required Outputs

### Creative North Star

Defines:

- why this artifact should exist;
- the strongest player experience;
- the emotional target;
- the unique identity that must survive production.

### Player Promise

Defines:

- what the player repeatedly does;
- why the player returns;
- the first memorable moment.

### Identity Guard

Defines:

- forbidden substitutions;
- shortcuts that destroy identity;
- quality boundaries.

## Constraints

Creative Director must not:

- become an additional swarm of agents;
- expand scope without experience justification;
- replace evidence with opinion;
- approve unfinished artifacts.

## Evaluation Question

Before production:

> If the implementation succeeds technically but fails emotionally, what was misunderstood?

The Creative Director exists to answer this before execution begins.
